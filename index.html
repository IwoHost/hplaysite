01

What Hplay is

Most music apps want an account, a subscription and a connection. Hplay wants a folder.

You point it at music that is already on your device and it plays it, with a wheel you spin instead of a list you scroll. Cover art, albums, artists, playlists, search, a sleep timer, a vinyl view that spins while the song runs, and five different screen looks. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There is no sign-in, because there is nothing to sign in to.

It behaves like a music app should on a phone. It holds the lock screen with the cover art on it, puts controls in the notification shade, takes the volume keys, pauses when you pull your headphones out, and keeps playing with the screen off.

This is an alpha

Hplay is still being built and tested. Things move, break and get renamed between versions. Keep your own copy of your music, do not treat the player as the only place your playlists live, and tell me when something goes wrong. That is what the alpha is for.

Once you have it

Step one

Install it

Your phone will ask whether you trust an app from outside the store. Say yes, and it installs like any other. Nothing to register, no account to make.

Step two

Add music

Point Hplay at a folder of your own files, or add songs one at a time. It reads the tags and the cover art out of the files themselves.

Step three

Spin the wheel

Drag around the wheel to move, press the middle to choose, press MENU to go back. A short guided tour runs the first time so you are not guessing.

No music handy? Take the twenty demo tracks below, drop them in a folder, and point the player at that.

06

Questions

What is Hplay?

Hplay is a music player for Android that plays the music files already stored on your phone. Instead of a list you scroll, it is driven by a wheel: you turn the ring with your thumb to move through a list and press the middle to choose. It needs no account and no internet connection.

Does Hplay work offline?

Yes, entirely. Hplay never needs a connection. Your music is read from the device, and so is the cover art, which comes out of the tags inside the files rather than from an online lookup. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is downloaded while you listen.

Does Hplay need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, because there is nothing to sign in to. There is no account, no subscription, no ads and no tracking of any kind. Your listening statistics are counted on the device and stay there.

Which audio formats does Hplay play?

Whatever the phone itself can decode, which on Android means mp3, flac, m4a and aac, ogg, opus and wav at the least. Cover art is read from ID3 tags in mp3 files, Vorbis comments in flac, ogg and opus, and the ilst atom in m4a.

How does Hplay handle playlists?

A folder is a playlist. Point Hplay at a folder of music and every folder inside it becomes a playlist named after that folder, whatever the tags inside the files say. Renaming a folder renames the playlist, and the order of songs within one can be set by hand and is remembered.

Who makes Hplay?

One person: Iwo Dec, working as HostLabo. It is written by hand, with no framework and no build step, and it is not open source. Hplay is copyright © 2026 Iwo Dec, all rights reserved. You can read the full licence, which is short.

Is Hplay free?

Yes. Free, with no paid tier, no ads and nothing to buy inside it. The twenty demo tracks on this site are free to download and free to use as well.

How do I get Hplay?

Hplay is a closed alpha, so it is handed out one person at a time rather than downloaded from a store. Email hostlabosupport@gmail.com and the app is sent back to you to install yourself. There is no waiting list and no form.

Is Hplay on the Google Play Store?

Not yet. While it is in alpha it is distributed by email as an APK you install yourself. That is also why the version numbers move quickly and why things change between builds.

What does a scroll wheel player mean?

The main control is a ring you turn with your thumb rather than a list you swipe. Turning moves the highlight; spinning faster jumps further; the middle opens what is highlighted; the outer edges are menu, play or pause, and skip. It is the way portable music players worked before touchscreens, rebuilt as an Android app.

Does Hplay show cover art?

Yes, read out of your own files with no connection involved. A song whose file has no artwork is given one of twenty sleeves drawn by the app, and it keeps the same one for good.

Accessibility

This page aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. Here is honestly where it stands.

What has been checked

Every piece of text on this site was measured against what is actually behind it, in the light theme and the dark one, and all of it clears the 4.5:1 contrast the standard asks for — including text over gradients, measured against the worst part of the gradient rather than an average. Everything you can operate has a name a screen reader can read out, a visible focus ring, and a target at least 24 by 24 pixels. Each page has one main heading, headings in order, a skip link, and proper landmarks. The page works at 320 pixels wide, which is what 400% zoom comes to, without anything being cut off or scrolling sideways. It survives a reader's own stylesheet forcing wider line, letter and word spacing. Nothing is conveyed by colour alone, animation respects a reduced-motion setting, and the light and dark themes both follow your system setting unless you change it here.

What has not

None of the above was tested with a real screen reader by somebody who uses one every day, and that is the test that counts. The photographs have written descriptions, but a description is somebody's opinion of a picture and mine may not be the useful one. The demo tracks are music with no words, so there are no captions or transcripts; if that is wrong, tell me. This is a one-person alpha, so it is checked by a script on every change rather than audited by anyone qualified.

The app itself

Hplay the Android app is a separate thing from this page and has not been through the same check. It has a text size setting, a high-contrast screen mode, a reduced-motion setting and a haptics switch, but it is driven by a scroll wheel, which is a demanding thing to operate. If it does not work for you I would genuinely like to know, because that is fixable and worth fixing.

Tell me when something does not work

Email hostlabosupport@gmail.com with what you were trying to do and what got in the way. You will get a reply from a person. If something here stops you using the page, say so and it goes to the front of the queue.
