If you want to support the work you can, but you never have to. People who do are thanked by name on a page in the app and on this site, and sent a code for a skin. Your music and your data

Your music stays on your device. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics and no tracking. The listening statistics are counted on the device, are never sent anywhere, and you can switch them off or wipe them in Settings › Statistics › Usage Data.
