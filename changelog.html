The numbering changed here

From v44.0.0 every version has three numbers, and which one moved says what kind of release it was. Everything below kept the two numbers it shipped with.

Major
Proud
The one that goes on the résumé. A real change, and a codename never used before.
Minor
Default
New things, and they were fine.
Patch
Shame
Fixes only. Please don’t ask.
