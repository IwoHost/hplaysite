Your Year in Hplay

This page draws a slideshow of your listening out of a link your player makes. There is nothing here until you give it one.

  1. On Hplay, open Settings → Statistics
  2. Choose Your Year, Wrapped
  3. Send the link to yourself and open it

Your listening never reaches a server. It rides in the part of the address after the #, which browsers keep to themselves. This page reads it on your own device and draws it there.
