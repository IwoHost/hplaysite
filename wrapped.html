Your Year
in Hplay

This page turns a link from your player into a slideshow of your year. There is nothing here until you give it one.

  1. On Hplay, open Settings › Statistics
  2. Choose Your Year, Wrapped
  3. Send the link to yourself and open it

Your listening never reaches a server. It rides in the part of the address after the #, which browsers keep to themselves, and it is drawn here, on your own device.
