Gold and Coffee are free now

Both skins come with every copy of Hplay from v44.1.0. No code, no link, nothing to redeem: they are in Settings › Look › Body Color, finishes and all.

Gold

Gold body, graphite wheel

Coffee

Crema body, espresso wheel

Open Hplay

They used to come with a coffee. A coffee buys nothing now, not even a colour: everybody gets the same Hplay, and the people who support it are thanked by name instead — in the app under About Hplay › Credits, and on the home page.

If you were sent a code like COFE#8762-K4QX-9PMZ, you do not need it any more. If you already had the skin, you keep it; it is just called Gold or Coffee now.
